Privacy Policy

Correct Scores Vault HT FT
Effective 8 October 2026 · Last updated 9 October 2026

1. Who this covers

This policy explains how the Correct Scores Vault HT FT Android app handles information. The app shows football tips. It is free to install and is only for adults aged 18 and over. We do not collect a contact email address.

2. What the app shows

Sections are Free Tips, BTTS, Over / Under, 5+ Odds, 2+ Odds, Correct Scores, HT FT, and History. Opening Free Tips, BTTS, Over / Under, 5+ Odds, 2+ Odds, or History can show a full-screen ad, and a banner can appear on those screens. HT FT and Correct Scores do not show those ads. Today's tips in HT FT and Correct Scores cost 20 coins the first time you open them on a day. History for HT FT and Correct Scores does not cost coins. Coins are earned by watching a rewarded ad, 20 coins per video, and you can watch more than one. The tip line for today's HT FT and Correct Scores games stays hidden. Those two screens also show buttons that open the Telegram channel and the WhatsApp channel. The app does not sell in-app purchases or subscriptions.

3. Information we collect

We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, postal address, or payment card. The app does not have an account you create.

Google services used by the app may process device model, operating system, language, advertising or app identifiers, and basic usage events. That includes:

  • Firebase Realtime Database, which stores the tip content
  • Firebase Analytics, which measures how the app is used
  • Firebase Authentication, which creates an anonymous technical ID
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging, which delivers notifications if you allow them
  • Google AdMob, when a banner, full-screen ad, or rewarded ad is shown or tapped

Coin balance and whether HT FT or Correct Scores was opened today are stored on your device. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes that local data.

4. How information is used

  • To show tips and history
  • To show ads and to grant coins after a rewarded ad
  • To send notifications you allow
  • To understand usage and fix problems
  • To keep the app limited to adults

5. Advertising

Google AdMob may serve banner ads, full-screen ads, and rewarded ads. Ad partners process data under their own policies. You can limit ad personalization in your Android settings. Google's privacy policy is at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

6. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information is shared with the Google services above, when the law requires it, or to protect the app and its users. Telegram and WhatsApp are optional links you can open yourself from HT FT and Correct Scores. They are outside this app: https://t.me/https0029VbE6BOvFcowCmqBFnj1J and WhatsApp channel.

7. Retention and security

Local preferences stay until you clear them or uninstall. Analytics, notification, and ad data follow Google's retention. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure.

8. Children

The app is not for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Your choices

You can turn notifications off, limit ad personalization, reset your advertising ID, clear app data, or uninstall. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or object to certain information. Because the app does not collect your email or a named account, those choices are handled through your device and Google account settings.

If you are in California, we do not sell personal information. If you are in the European Economic Area, access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and objection apply where they cover data that is actually held.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The date above will change. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
